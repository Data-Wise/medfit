## Errata for spec 4.5c (draft, not applied; decision D12)

The grammar spec `SPEC-sem-grammar-2026-10-08.md` is approved and frozen. The plan overrides the points below with numbered decisions (Q10, Q14) and cites this block. Amending the spec needs a separate explicit approval from the author.

1. **Bounds in the active set (4.5c, line 170).** Add: "Active box bounds join the active set: a lower bound `l_i` is the row `c = l_i - x_i` (column `-e_i`) and an upper bound `u_i` is the row `c = x_i - u_i` (column `+e_i`), each with a non-negative multiplier." Evidence: `s1-review/t2-bounds.out`, a Heywood fit with `lb = 0`: 4.5c as written gives KKT 0.366 (spurious rejection), the bound as `-e_i` gives 5.88e-09 (accept), `+e_i` gives 0.366.
2. **Scale-free stationarity (4.5c, line 170).** Replace the scaling `max(1, max|grad F|)` and the absolute threshold with: "stationarity is measured by a scale-free quantity (the Newton decrement on the reduced Hessian of the active-constraint null space), with the threshold calibrated across model structures and data scales, not an absolute residual." Evidence: `s1-review/t12-scale-extremes.out`, at data x1000 30 of 30 stalled fits are accepted (up to 22 SE off), at x0.01 8 of 30 converged fits are rejected.
3. **Scaled windows (4.5c).** The `1e-6` feasibility tolerance and the active-set window `c(x) >= -1e-6` are scaled by `max(1, the constraint-gradient norm)`.
4. **Starts inside the box (4.5b, line 158).** Perturbed starts are clamped into `[lb, ub]` before the positive-definiteness validity check. Evidence: `s1-review/t2-bounds.out`, 42 of 1000 perturbed starts passed the check but violated `lb`, and nloptr then errors.
