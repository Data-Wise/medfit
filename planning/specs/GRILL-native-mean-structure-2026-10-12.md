## Decision Ledger

| # | Branch | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| G1 (pre-answered, shown for override) | Is the mean/covariance information a "cross block"? | **No: it is additive over the same theta.** Under normality `I_jk = dmu_j' Sigma^-1 dmu_k + (1/2) tr(Sigma^-1 dSigma_j Sigma^-1 dSigma_k)`; there is no mean-covariance cross term. Evidence: `.sem_info_expected` (`R/sem-ml.R:238`) is the covariance part only, so it needs the mean term added. The BRAINSTORM's "cross block" wording and its planted defect "zero the information cross block" are replaced by "omit the mean term from the information". Scores likewise gain an additive `dmu' Sigma^-1 (x_i - mu)` term. |
| G2 | Release shape: one release or split? (weakest recommendation) | **Split: observed first, latent later** (recommended). Latent mean/intercept rows keep a clear "not yet supported" error until lavaan's identification rules are verified (open question 1). Supersedes the SPEC's all-phases-in-0.9.0 target. |
| G3 | How do covariance-only fits stay untouched? (implementation regret) | **One code path gated by a `has_mean` flag, plus a gate:** F, gradient, SEs, and logLik on the existing frozen and OpenMx fixtures match 0.8.0 to 1e-12 with the flag off (recommended). Consequence: edits to `.sem_eval`/`.sem_scores`/`.sem_sample` are covered bit for bit; no second copy of the numerics. |
| G4 | Four-way via native: this release or follow-up? (benefit honesty) | **Pulled forward** (recommended was: proceed with an honest NEWS and one constraint example). Reverses brainstorm decision 5. Honest benefit statement still required: in a saturated-mean fit paths, NIE, NDE, and SEs equal today's; the payoff is constrained intercepts and four-way. Four-way needs only observed intercepts (E[M \| X = 0]), so it does not wait for latent means. |
| G5 | X×M representation for four-way | **Add a `:` product operator to the native grammar** (recommended was: a user-supplied product column). This amends the frozen `SPEC-sem-grammar-2026-10-08.md`; the amendment needs its own approval before any parser work (see Open Questions). |
| G6 | Sequencing (reversibility and scope creep) | **Three releases:** 0.9.0 observed mean structure (SPEC phases 1-3, 5, 6); 0.10.0 grammar amendment, `:` operator, four-way via native; 0.11.0 latent means (SPEC phase 4). Each gated by its own oracle (recommended). Supersedes the SPEC's single 0.9.0 target. |
| G7 (verified 2026-10-12) | Which `~1` rows does lavaan fix or free for latent variables under `meanstructure = TRUE`? (gates 0.11.0; was open question 1) | **Probe on lavaan 0.7.2, script `evidence/native-mean-structure-2026-10-12/lavaan-latent-means.R`, results:** (A) with `meanstructure = TRUE` and no `~1` written, every indicator intercept is **free**, latent intercepts/means (`M`, `Y`) are **fixed at 0**, and the exogenous mean is fixed at the sample mean (`fixed.x = TRUE`), df 12. (B) a free `M ~ 1` with all indicator intercepts free is **unidentified**: lavaan fits and then warns "could not compute standard errors, information matrix could not be inverted". (C) a free `M ~ 1` with one indicator intercept fixed (`m1 ~ 0*1`) is identified, df 12 (the latent mean replaces that indicator's intercept), `M` = -0.069 equals the earlier `m1` intercept. (D) an explicit `M ~ 0*1` equals (A). (E) writing any `~1` (here `m1 ~ 1`) without `meanstructure` switches mean structure on and equals (A); (F) a lone free `M ~ 1` does the same and is unidentified like (B). (G) `std.lv = TRUE` and (H) free marker loading with fixed latent variance leave the mean rows as in (A). **Consequences for 0.11.0:** default completion matches (A) (latent means fixed 0, indicator intercepts free); a free latent `~1` must come with a fixed or constrained indicator intercept per latent variable, and the engine should **error up front** naming the latent variable (lavaan only warns after the fit), as a sibling of `.sem_check_scale()`; a generic local-identification check (rank of the moment Jacobian at the start) is the fallback for constrained cases [A, not yet tested]; the lavaan oracle for observed-variable means must use `fixed.x = FALSE` because lavaan's default fixes the exogenous mean at the sample mean. Single run, one model family, one seed. |
| G8 (2026-10-13) | Where is the `anova()` nesting bug (found by Codex review, reproduced) fixed? | **A separate fix PR first**: `.sem_anova_nested` ignored the larger model's constraints and fixed values, so `a == b` against `a == 0, b == 0.5` returned a p-value. Rule: linear equalities by implication (restricted equalities plus fixed values span them), nonlinear and inequality rows verbatim, fixed values equal. Done on `fix/anova-nesting` (`8742cce`, not pushed). T5 builds on it. |
| G9 (2026-10-13, H-A) | Gate and SEs when intercept mean/sd is large | **Centered parameterization plus scaled starts.** Information, inversion and the identification check run in implied-mean coordinates (phi = paths, variances, mu) and map back exactly (`V = J V_phi J'`); the same centering in `.sem_gate_projected` and `.sem_active_set`; sd floor and sample-mean starts for scale (latents: mu0 = 0, so `m_free = xbar_v - (A0 mu0)_v`). Evidence (Opus review prototype): Jacobi-scaled eigenvalue is unchanged by floor or starts (4.8e-3 vs 4.5e-3 at mean/sd 10); recalibrating `.sem_singular_tol` would have to drop below about 4.5e-7 while S6's degenerate points sit at 1.7e-5; centered eigenvalue 0.47-0.61 from mean/sd 0 to 1e5; `.sem_invert_info` scaled rcond 1.5e-8 at 3000 and every SE becomes NA from about 4500. |
| G10 (2026-10-13, H-C) | A fixed `y ~ 0*1` is dropped by 0.8.0 (df 0) and a restriction under 0.9.0 (df 1) | **Announced correctness change.** NEWS behavior-change entry (CLAUDE.md correctness exemption: minor bump), `meanstructure` tri-state (`NULL`: on when any `~1` is written; `FALSE` with a free `~1` errors), a frozen intended-change fixture, and the tests and help text that encode the old behavior listed in T2. |
| G11 (2026-10-13, M-A) | Parser: accept lavaan's `y ~ 1 + x`? | **Accept it in 0.9.0.** The native parser rejects it today ("an intercept must be its own statement"), so the lavaan-comparison row could never flip. This amends the frozen grammar: the SPEC amendment ([SPEC-sem-grammar-amendment-intercept-term-2026-10-13.md](SPEC-sem-grammar-amendment-intercept-term-2026-10-13.md)) was **approved 2026-10-13** with D1 (a repeated `1` errors) and D2 (either position); T2's parser work may proceed. |
| G12 (2026-10-13) | Shared label between an intercept and a path | **Allowed** (reverses the plan's refusal). The refusal's reason (`gmap` has only A and S columns) is weak because `gmap` gains mean columns anyway. The step-floor concern becomes a test (mixed-scale shared parameter), not an error. |
| G13 (2026-10-13) | `fixed_x` (RMediation request) and the "experimental" label | **Build `fixed_x` (Option C: `FALSE`, `TRUE` or variable names), 0.9.1 after 0.9.0 T5, default `FALSE`**; plan: [PLAN-native-fixed-x](PLAN-native-fixed-x-2026-10-13.md). **Target 0.11.0 for dropping "experimental"** (told to RMediation; the criteria, and whether `fixed_x` counts toward it, are not defined). medfit stays GitHub-only until a CRAN decision is made separately. |
